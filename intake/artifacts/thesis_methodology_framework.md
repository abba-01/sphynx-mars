# Thesis Framework: Cross-Planetary Sphinx Communication Hypothesis

## **CENTRAL THESIS STATEMENT**

The Mars sphinx and Earth Sphinx represent either:
1. **Unified Design**: Created by the same architect/civilization, or  
2. **Response Communication**: One created in direct response to the other as a form of interplanetary communication

The transmission/receiving directionality is unknown, but the geometric correlation demonstrates intentional correspondence requiring advanced knowledge of both planetary sites.

- **Artifact Size Determination**:

  - Historical survey data for verification

#### **1.3 Projection Standardization**
- **Orientation**: Both sites projected with directional north at top
- **Scale**: Normalized to enable direct geometric comparison
- **Coordinate System**: Universal reference frame for angle/distance measurements
