**Visual Attention Narrowing:**
Human vision naturally tunnels under cognitive load, focusing visual attention on a narrow central region while excluding peripheral information. **Stress-Induced Tunneling:**
Under stress or high cognitive load, all senses become "tunneled," with visual attention focused on a small geographic area while missing peripheral information. This represents an adaptive mechanism to concentrate processing resources on the most relevant stimuli.
